WHAT IT IS

One live wall for the world’s cybersecurity reporting.

CyberShield is a self-hosted threat-intelligence dashboard. It pulls cybersecurity news from 19 trusted research streams into a single real-time feed — automatically scoring every story for severity, classifying it into threat categories, and explaining what it means on demand with an AI analyst verdict.
